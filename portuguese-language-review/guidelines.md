# Portuguese Language Review Guidelines

## Objective

The objective of this exercise is to review Portuguese text for grammar,
clarity, naturalness, instruction-following, and regional-language suitability.

## Review Criteria

### Grammar

Check spelling, agreement, verb forms, punctuation, and sentence structure.

### Clarity

Check whether the meaning is understandable, direct, and free from unnecessary
ambiguity.

### Naturalness

Check whether the text sounds natural to a Portuguese speaker in the intended
context.

### Instruction Following

Check whether the response answers the user's request and follows the requested
format, tone, and language.

### Regional Usage

Identify whether wording belongs to a Portuguese regional variety, such as
Mozambican Portuguese, European Portuguese, or Brazilian Portuguese. A regional
difference is not automatically an error.

## Decision Rules

1. Review the full text before assigning a quality score.
2. Do not treat an accepted regional variation as a grammar error.
3. Mark grammar errors only when they make the text incorrect according to
   standard Portuguese rules.
4. Distinguish between an incorrect phrase and a phrase that is simply less
   natural in a particular region.
5. Use factual and constructive reviewer notes.
6. Do not infer the writer's intention beyond the available text.
7. Assign one overall quality score from 1 to 5.

## Quality Scale

- **5 — Excellent:** Correct, clear, natural, and fully appropriate.
- **4 — Good:** Mostly correct, with a small issue that does not affect meaning.
- **3 — Fair:** Understandable, but contains relevant grammar, clarity, or
  naturalness issues.
- **2 — Poor:** Contains multiple issues or only partially follows the request.
- **1 — Unacceptable:** Incorrect, unclear, irrelevant, unsafe, or does not
  follow the request.
